      06 — Gully rules

      Kyunki gully cricket ka apna constitution hota hai.

      Rule 01

      Blue ghar mein ball jaaye — out.

      Rule 02

      Baaki gharon mein ball jaaye — 2 run.

      Rule 03

      Ball naali mein gayi — 2 run.

      Rule 04

      Bhaiya ke ramp se ball cross ho jaaye — boundary.
